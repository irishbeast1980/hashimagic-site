About

Helping people with Hashimoto’s or thyroid conditions see the whole picture

Our mission

Hashimagic exists to make day-to-day life with Hashimoto’s and thyroid conditions clearer. We bring the details together in one clear record, and weigh general information by the strength of its evidence, bridging conventional medicine and lifestyle approaches with care.

Where we are

Hashimagic is in development and being tested now. Launch is coming. Founded in the UK.

Founder

Stephen

Hashimagic was founded by Stephen, who brings 15 years of NHS IT, project management and software development experience, inspired by his partner’s experience of living with Hashimoto’s.

What we stand for

  • Evidence first. Stronger evidence carries more weight.
  • Respect for clinicians. We support care, never replace it.
  • Calm by design. A little each day is enough.
