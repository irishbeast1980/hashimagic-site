The app

Try Hashimagic. Help shape it.

Hashimagic is a phone-first app that helps people living with Hashimoto’s and other thyroid conditions keep a clear record of their everyday lives, bringing medication, supplements, diet, exercise, symptoms and weight together in one calm place.

Beta

Join the mission: help us shape Hashimagic

We’d love you to try the beta and tell us what you think. It isn’t a finished product yet, and every tester makes it better.

Open the app Become a tester

At present, one tap install is only available on Android. Apple doesn’t currently allow web apps to install automatically on iPhone, so iPhone users need to follow the iPhone guide. The Android help is just a fallback if the Install button doesn’t appear.

iPhone guideAndroid help (if Install doesn’t appear)

  • You don’t need Hashimoto’s to test. Just choose Hashimoto’s as your diagnosis.
  • Use the same browser and link each time, because your diary is saved in your browser.
  • Feedback is welcome by email, and every bug or suggestion helps shape the app.
