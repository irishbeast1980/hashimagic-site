Phase two

What’s being considered for phase two

These ideas are being explored only. Nothing here is promised, and there is no timetable.

  • Cloud sync so your diary can follow you across devices.
  • An install banner that makes adding Hashimagic to your Home screen clearer.
  • Backup and restore for your local diary.
  • A quick test mode for trying the app without a full setup.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store as possible distribution routes.
  • Automatic blood review: exploring automatically reviewing uploaded blood results as general information only, not a diagnosis.
